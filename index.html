About

My background and interests.

I am an Informatics graduate of Universitas Sebelas Maret (UNS), Faculty of Information Technology and Data Science (2022-2026, GPA 3.78), with a strong technical foundation in IT support, networking fundamentals, database management, and IoT and microcontroller concepts, acquired through professional certifications and reinforced by hands-on application development.

As an Application Developer Intern at the Dinas Komunikasi, Informatika, Statistik, dan Persandian (Diskominfo SP) of Surakarta City, I developed and maintained applications in accordance with project requirements, assisted in implementing and testing features, and resolved application issues to improve functionality and performance. Previously, I completed the Machine Learning cohort at Bangkit Academy (MSIB), specializing in Supervised Learning and TensorFlow, where I developed predictive models in Python.

My academic projects include a web-based boarding house rental system designed using UML and a court database management system modeled with an ERD and a relational data model. My final project, a modified VGA-Net for retinal blood vessel segmentation, was published as a journal article. I also hold certifications in IT support (IBM), network security (Coursera), IoT (University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign), and data analytics (Google).

I am proficient in application services, database systems, and IT operational support, and I adapt quickly to structured field and on-site IT service environments.
